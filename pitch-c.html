Quick answers

What founders ask us.

Who actually employs the agent?+

You do — directly, as an independent contractor. After placement we step out completely. No layer between you, no markup on their pay.

What does the agent's salary cost me?+

That's between you and them — each profile shows the candidate's asking rate up front. Most support and admin agents ask $700–$1,200 a month, paid directly to them via Wise or Payoneer. We never touch it.

Are the agents fluent in English and Spanish?+

Yes — every agent works in both English and Spanish. English is tested live on video before anyone reaches your shortlist.

What if it doesn't work out?+

One message within 45 days and we place a replacement free. No forms. No questions. Your placement fee keeps working until the seat is filled right.

Why are you so much cheaper than the others?+

No office, no commissioned recruiters, a flat fee instead of a % of salary — HireLATAM charges $3,500 for the same placement. The model is lean. The screening isn't — every finalist is interviewed live on video. And we're not guessing: we built and ran our own call team in Colombia for a major US telecom before opening our bench to clients.

When do I pay, and which tier should I pick?+

One flat fee per placement — no deposits, no add-ons. Most clients pick VETTED ($1,997): it's everything on this page, including the psych report and the 45-day guarantee. SOURCE ($1,497) skips the deep-vet extras; MANAGED adds $149/mo for us to stay on after placement. Team pricing on the call.

How are you different from HireLATAM?+

Same vetted placement for $1,500 less — HireLATAM charges $3,500; our VETTED tier is $1,997 (from $1,497). One flat fee instead of a percentage of salary, a licensed-psychologist evaluation on every finalist, and you employ and pay the agent directly — we never take a cut of their pay.

Can I hire a bilingual virtual assistant or executive assistant?+

Yes — bilingual virtual assistants and executive assistants are core roles we place, alongside customer support, inside sales / SDR, and admin and ops. Every agent works in both English and Spanish and is screened the same way.

What countries are the agents from?+

Colombia, Venezuela, Mexico, Peru, Ecuador, and Argentina. We source across all six Latin American markets to find the best bilingual fit for your role and time zone.

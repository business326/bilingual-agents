FAQ

Questions, answered

How fast can an agent start?
Typically 7 days from the role call: days 2–5 we source and screen, day 6 you get your shortlist, day 7 they start.
When do I pay, and which tier?
One flat fee per placement, no deposits. Vetted ($1,997) includes the psych report and the 45-day guarantee. Source ($1,497) skips those extras; Managed adds $149/mo for us to stay on after placement.
Do the agents really speak English?
Yes — and you verify it yourself. Every finalist does a live video interview, plus a recorded 5-minute intro. No English levels on paper.
What is the psychologist screening?
A licensed psychologist evaluates temperament, communication and reliability, and hands you a written report. It separates someone who interviews well from someone who performs long-term.
What if it doesn't work out?
You have 45 days of free replacement. If your agent isn't a fit, we place another at no cost. No contracts, no monthly fees (unless you choose Managed).
How does the agent get paid?
You pay the agent directly, with $0 markup from us, ever. Most ask $700–$1,200/mo. Our only fee is the one-time placement (plus $149/mo on Managed).
